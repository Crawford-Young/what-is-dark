## Layout

- Pure gameplay math lives in `RefCounted` scripts under `scripts/logic/` (`source_move.gd`, `yaw_drain.gd`, `wallrun_logic.gd`, `erosion_logic.gd`, `loop_logic.gd`, `void_logic.gd`, `posture.gd`, `course_logic.gd`, `course_spec.gd`, `zone_spec.gd`) — full TDD, 100% coverage expectation. `source_move.gd` + `yaw_drain.gd` are the GAME's movement core (adopted from the momentum lab, phase 3a); `move_math.gd` is deleted.
- Node scripts (`scripts/player.gd`, `scripts/course.gd`, `scripts/slice.gd`, `scripts/zone.gd`, `scripts/kitchen.gd`, `scripts/swallow_director.gd`, `scripts/void_wall.gd`, lab-only `scripts/momentum_player.gd`/`momentum_lab.gd`) wire input/scene-tree only and delegate all math to `scripts/logic/`.
- **Course binding:** `Course` (`scripts/course.gd`, abstract) owns all course gameplay wiring — erosion, the void, the catch, fall resets, the door, respawns — and reads per-course data from a `CourseSpec` (`scripts/logic/course_spec.gd`: scene path, declarations path, markers, door progress, `fall_y`, `void_start`, audit baseline) that the subclass supplies via `_build_spec()`. `Slice extends Course`; its constants are its spec (`Slice.spec()`). Blender-exported places are `Zone extends Course` (`scripts/zone.gd`): it builds `Art` (the glb), `PLAY/<box>` bodies and `CoursePath` from the sidecar, parsed and validated by pure `ZoneSpec` (`scripts/logic/zone_spec.gd`); `Kitchen` (`scripts/kitchen.gd`, `scenes/kitchen.tscn`, `just kitchen`) is place 2, its constants its spec like the slice.
- Dev tooling lives in `scripts/dev/` (`course_audit.gd` survey collector, `audit_cli.gd` CLI entry) — scene-bound headless tooling, neither pure `scripts/logic/` math nor a gameplay node script. `CourseAudit` takes a live `Course`, so it cannot be pure; it still gets GUT coverage like logic.
- **Momentum lab** (`scenes/momentum_lab.tscn`, `just momentum-lab`): Source-movement evaluation env, verdict ADOPT 2026-07-24 — core adopted into `scripts/player.gd` in phase 3a; lab scene kept as a tuning sandbox. Movement constants live ONLY in `scripts/logic/source_move.gd` (top-of-file) and are game-wide; two deliberate CS2-parity breaks are user-locked there (`JUMP_SPEED` airtime parity, `AIR_CAP` 1.5 = 2× turn window).
- **Erodable-platform contract** (erosion v3, ruling #14): a `StaticBody3D` in group `erodable` (authored in `slice.tscn`, or built by `Zone` from a sidecar box's `erode` record) with children `Col` (CollisionShape3D/BoxShape3D) + `Mesh` (MeshInstance3D/BoxMesh) and metadata `erode_axis` (0=x, 2=z), `erode_min` (plateau size on that axis, m), `erode_anchor` (−1 pin low edge / 0 centre / +1 pin high edge). `course.gd:_cache_erodable()` reads the group ONCE at `_ready` (only bodies under that course) and duplicates the shared sub-resources per body (a shape is shared across platforms — `Sh1FloorB` and `Sh3FloorA` both use `shape_f14` — so erosion needs a private copy or it resizes untagged siblings); `course.gd:apply_erosion()` then recomputes size+origin from that cached shipped geometry every loop (idempotent; loop 0 = shipped course exactly). NEVER tag kickers, surf ramps, ride walls, marker/respawn floors, feed platforms, or the door — issue #12 + the E2 max-erosion audit are the standing law.
- **Perforated floors** (erosion E4 Grief/Depression, E5 Shame, E6 chasms): `D6Tile*` (9), `G5Tile*` (9), `Sh6ExitTile*` (8), `Sh8FloorTile*` (8), `D1Tile*` (7) and the finger triples `G2Pad*`/`G8PadA*`/`G8PadB*` are ONE logical floor authored as a grid of ordinary `erodable` tiles — no new logic, no new metadata. Long floors split along z into lateral lanes and shrink on `erode_axis` 2 with anchors `+1 / 0 / −1` (outer tiles pin the floor's outer edge, so the junctions to the neighbouring platforms never move; a two-tile lane uses `+1 / −1`); the short wide pads split along x and shrink on `erode_axis` 0. Every lane carries at least one hole — a hole-free lane is a hold-W bypass. **The binding invariant is loop-0 contiguity:** at `world_scale(0) = 0` the tiles must abut exactly and reproduce the shipped solid box, so a tile's `size` and `position` may only be changed as a pair that preserves the seam. A junction's opening is the SUM of the two neighbouring tiles' pullbacks at that seam; for a tile of shrink `s` and anchor `a` the pullback is `(1−a)·s/2` at its high-z edge and `(1+a)·s/2` at its low-z edge, so the split across a seam may be asymmetric while the sum stays the authored width. Perforation REPLACES width-shrink on a surface — never stack both (issue #23). Never perforate the surfaces on the standing no-tag list below, and never open a hole under a prop (beds, dressers, stands, gurneys), a marker respawn point (all markers sit at x = 0, the middle lane), or a mandatory drop/surf landing band — verify per floor.
- **Two hole grades on a perforated floor** (E6, user pick "full-width chasms — must jump"): **staggered holes** open 2.5 m per side, one lane at a time at different z, so **hole length in metres = loop number** (1 m at loop 1, 5 m at plateau) — avoidable by sidestepping, they supply weave/texture, and ≥2 lanes stay viable at any z. **Chasms** align EVERY lane's seam at one z with the SAME per-side pullbacks, so the floor parts edge to edge and the gap can only be jumped: **1.5 m per loop, 7.5 m at plateau**, against an 8.31 m flat reach at `RUN_SPEED` — 0.81 m is the entire margin and 7.5 m is a hard ceiling. A chasm deliberately breaks the ≥2-viable-lines law; that is the mechanic, not an oversight (0 % of plateau resets came from sidesteppable holes). Shipped chasms: `Sh6Exit` z −430 seam (lips −427.5 / −435), `Sh8Floor` −526 (−523.5 / −531), `G5` −924 (−920.25 / −927.75), `D1` −1240.5 (−1236.75 / −1244.25), `D6` −1590.5 (−1585.5 / −1593). Chasm laws, all verified per chasm: enough solid runway from the nearest standing marker respawn to reach `RUN_SPEED` (~1 m of ground accel); **≥3 m of FULL-WIDTH solid floor past the far lip** — a chasm landing into a staggered hole is the issue-#23 compound trap and is forbidden; never open one under a marker, prop, ramp, ride wall, kicker or the door; and counting chasms plus the zone's existing committed gaps, never more than 3 consecutive void-punished precision jumps (ruling #7). Verify chasm geometry by instantiating the scene and reading it live at every loop 0–5 — never hand-math — with a negative control (chasm centre NOT covered at plateau, covered at loop 0). **A second, independent verification must differ in METHOD, not just in authorship**: two probes both reading `BoxShape3D.size` share a blind spot (any OTHER collider silently filling a chasm reads as open), so the confirming pass raycasts the live physics space instead. **Every probe reports its own sample size each run** — a window edge that drifts off the floor, or into a staggered hole, silently shrinks coverage while still printing a confident-looking result; a sample count that changes between loops is investigated before the numbers are trusted (both failure modes hit the E6 verification pass).
- **No coverage gate on node scripts/scenes.** Stated explicitly so this is never mistaken for an oversight — see games-domain CLAUDE.md, scene/node tests are smoke-level only.

## Test command

`just test` — run FOREGROUND, unpiped. Read the GUT summary block yourself (`Totals:` line — scripts/tests/asserts counts); never trust exit code alone.

**CI enforces the same read** (`.github/workflows/ci.yml`, pinned to Godot 4.7.1): it greps for `could not find script`, asserts the `Scripts` count against an `EXPECTED_SCRIPTS` floor, and asserts `Passing Tests == Tests` — because an exit-code-only gate passes a suite that silently shrank. **Adding a test script means bumping `EXPECTED_SCRIPTS` in the workflow env block** (currently 21); CI fails loudly if you forget, which is the intended direction. The launch step fails on any `ERROR:`/`WARNING:`/`SCRIPT ERROR` in 600 headless frames.

## Course-law harness (phase 4.0)

`just audit [course]` (default `slice`; names registered in `audit_cli.gd` `courses()`, an unknown name exits 2) walks the live course scene against its declared intent at every erosion loop 0–`PLATEAU_LOOP`, both survey methods, and exits 0 only when the deduped `law:subject` finding set EQUALS the course spec's `baseline` — for the slice `Slice.BASELINE_FINDINGS` (46 keys, every one inline-commented with an issue number or a named ruling), for the kitchen `Kitchen.BASELINE_FINDINGS`. Read the output, not just the exit code: per-pass `pass method=… loop=… samples=…` lines, then findings, then `findings=… unique=… baseline=…`.

- **Four modules, split by purity.** `scripts/logic/course_decl.gd` (declaration schema + role legality), `scripts/logic/course_law.gd` (laws as pure arithmetic), `scripts/logic/course_sweep.gd` (survey + declarations in, findings out) are pure `RefCounted` — full TDD. `scripts/dev/course_audit.gd` (the collector) takes a live `Course` and does all the looking — box-metadata survey and physics-ray survey — so it is dev tooling, not `scripts/logic/`. `scripts/dev/audit_cli.gd` is the `just audit` entry.
- **Declarations** (`CourseDecl.DECLARATIONS`): `bare_run`, `carried_speed`, `wall_ride`, `chasm`, `surf_transfer`, `gate_deploy`, `barrier` (a gap built NOT to be crossed: it must stay wider than `FLAT_REACH` at every loop; no landing laws, no precision-chain link — #23 kitchen ruling). **Roles** (`CourseDecl.ROLES`): `floor`, `kicker`, `surf_ramp`, `ride_wall`, `marker_floor`, `feed_platform`, `prop`, `door`, `gate_path`, `ceiling`. `gate_deploy` and `gate_path` are declarative-only at 4.0 — **gate laws are deferred to 4.1**; `ceiling` likewise carries no 4.0 law (4.1's lowering-ceiling verbs attach there).
- **Fixture manifest**: `data/course_declarations.json` — every traversal span names its verb, every surface its role. Zones keep spans (and any floors / landing bands / non-role surface keys) in `data/course/0N-<place>.declarations.json` instead; their roles come only from the sidecar, merged in by `ZoneSpec.manifest()`. An undeclared span is itself a finding (the issue-#32 defence: no span's real length can outgrow its recorded number unnoticed). Derive nothing the manifest states; assume nothing that can be read (capsule off the player's shape, erosion off `apply_erosion()`, shipped span off loop 0).
- **FAIL vs WARN** (`CourseSweep`): FAIL means a law was broken; WARN means a human has to rule — the five shipped WARN classes are `compound_landing` (gap-and-width compounds), `precision_chain_at_cap` (ruling-#7 cap), `surf_exit_unmeasured`, `wall_chain_gap` (the chain walk cannot see below-launch contact assists), and `ramp_band` (angle outside the measured surf band). The harness makes the class visible; it never overturns a ruling already made.
- **Two-method rule**: box read and ray grid are compared at `CourseLaw.METHOD_TOLERANCE` (0.10 m = 2× the 0.05 m ray step), never at `EPS` — two independent reads must agree or neither is evidence.
- **Sample counts are part of the evidence.** Every pass prints its own sample count. The box count is loop-invariant (467 every loop of the T9 baseline run) unless the course's spec records a ruled drop (the kitchen's at loop 5, commented beside `Kitchen.BASELINE_FINDINGS`); the ray count grows with erosion as holes open (5000 → 7397 across loops 0–5, same run). Any other change between loops is investigated before the numbers are trusted — a drifting probe window shrinks coverage while still printing confident results.
- **Changing the baseline** means a ruling: a new finding is a course or harness defect to fix or adjudicate (issue number + verdict in the issue log), never a key to append silently.

## Blender → Godot pipeline (#23)

A place is built in Blender and ridden in Godot; the files are the reference, this is the route.

1. **Builder tags** — `art/course/build/<place>.py` on `wid_common.py`: `box()` / `boxes_object()` take `role=` and `erode=(axis, min, anchor)` and record each box's loop-0 centre and size in the `wid_boxes` prop — the collider source; nothing is scanned from the mesh. `explicit_colliders()` gives a non-box PLAY mesh hand-laid boxes. The `COURSE` collection holds `SPAWN`, `MARKER_n`, `DOOR` and the `LINE` curve.
2. **`just export-course`** (`export_all.sh`: `selftest.py`, then `run.py <place> export` → `export.py`) fails loudly on any invalid PLAY object (untagged mesh, record ≠ mesh island, non-Bevel modifier, illegal role or erosion, a marker off its `marker_floor`), then writes `assets/course/0N-<place>.glb` (LFS) and sidecar v1 `data/course/0N-<place>.json`. Both are in the Godot frame (−bx, bz − top, by); the x flip negates `erode.anchor` on axis 0, in the exporter. Markers and the door are arc length along `LINE`. Re-exporting rewrites every place: the school's `Coat` displace is non-deterministic, so when only `Coat` differs, restore its glb and `.blend` from HEAD.
3. **`ZoneSpec` → `Zone`** — `scripts/logic/zone_spec.gd` validates the sidecar + declarations (every defect listed, nothing defaulted); `scripts/zone.gd` builds `Art`, `PLAY/<box>` and `CoursePath`, strips the glb meshes of eroding objects and redraws their boxes. The door is a progress threshold, never a box. A place is a `Zone` subclass whose constants are its spec (`scripts/kitchen.gd`).
4. **Declarations** — `data/course/0N-<place>.declarations.json`, keyed by sidecar box names: spans only (plus floors / landing bands if any). A role there is an error.
5. **`just audit <place>`** — register the place in `audit_cli.gd` `courses()`; its baseline lives in its spec. The span model reads straight −z gaps, so lateral crossings (the kitchen's chasm) need their own live two-method probe.

## Gotchas

- New `class_name` scripts need one `godot --headless --editor --quit --path .` pass (same Godot exe the Justfile's `godot` variable points at) before headless GUT will see them (rebuilds Godot's global script-class cache). **No Justfile recipe currently wraps this** — `just import` runs `--headless --path . --import`, which reimports assets, not the same flag combination, and does not rebuild the class cache. Run the editor-quit pass manually (or open/quit via `just edit`) whenever a new `class_name` script is added.
- Run `just import` after any `.tscn` edit.
- `art/.gdignore` keeps Godot out of the Blender sources: without it the headless import hits the native `.blend` importer, which errors with no Blender path set and leaves the course glbs unimported.

## Design constraint

"Speedrunning feel" is a standing course-design constraint: fast, hard, contained. HARD is commitment-first (user ruling 2026-07-22): gaps near the reach ceiling, precision landings, clear only at full execution — never margin-first "safely makeable" bands. Wall ride/jump is a MANDATORY core traversal verb (user ruling 2026-07-22, supersedes the earlier optional-only rule): zones contain wall-only sections (rides, climbs, wall-to-wall corridors), placed between markers (frozen respawn y=1 keeps marker floors low). Shell/boundary walls stay broken ≤25 m (rib/recess/gap) so the wall-jump chain (measured 196 m/24 m) never yields a free zone skip; designed wall sections are exempt but bounded ≤25 m. No auto-vault unless the geometry is stair-like.
